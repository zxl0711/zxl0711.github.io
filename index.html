Digit Burst

Digit Burst (数字风暴 / 數字風暴) is a single-player number-chain Boss battle game. Hold a number, drag through the next ones (3 → 4 → 5 …), and release to strike. Longer chains hit harder. Play Score Attack, Boss Challenge and the Weekly Challenge.
