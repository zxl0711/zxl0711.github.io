Privacy Policy — Digit Burst

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Digit Burst (数字风暴 / 數字風暴; the “Game”) and its independent developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handle information. It covers the Game on the Apple App Store (iOS) and, when released, Google Play (Android). The available features and service providers may differ by platform and version. Features described as conditional do not imply that they are active in every release. The Game is designed to be playable without a developer-operated account.

1. Local game data and information you provide

The Game stores settings, language preferences, game progress, local scores, gameplay records and related verification or pending leaderboard-submission records on your device. This information is used to run the Game, remember your preferences and maintain your records. To check results before they count, the Game keeps on your device a replay of recent runs (your inputs and their timing, the run's random seed and result), simple timing statistics such as the typical interval between links, and any pending leaderboard submissions, which include your Game Center player identifier. This information is never uploaded by the Game; only a qualifying score and its context are sent to Game Center. Local data is not automatically a developer-operated online account. You can generally remove it by deleting the Game or clearing app data where supported; device or cloud backups may retain copies.

We do not require your name, home address, phone number, contacts or precise device location to play. If you email us for support or privacy requests, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send so that we can respond.

The Game offers an optional feedback link to forms hosted by Tally (tally.so). Opening a form leaves the native gameplay interface and loads a third-party webpage. The Game does not attach a game account ID, device identifier, game seed or gameplay record to that link, and does not send the feedback text to Firebase. The form opened matches the Game's language setting. The forms do not ask for your name or email address. If you submit feedback, we can view the text and any other information you voluntarily include. Tally may also process technical information related to visiting and submitting the form, such as browser and usage information, in accordance with its Privacy Notice. Please do not include sensitive personal information. Contact us if you would like us to locate or delete a submission where possible; please tell us roughly when you submitted it and what it said, since the form is not linked to your game.

2. Advertising and advertising privacy choices

When ads are enabled, the Game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's advertising partners. On iOS, the Game shows only occasional full-screen interstitial ads, and only after a finished run when you tap PLAY AGAIN. Ads never appear during gameplay, are not shown in your first runs after launch, and are frequency-capped. The Game does not use rewarded ads. Other platform versions or future releases may have different ad formats, and this policy will be updated when necessary. After a verified ad-removal purchase, the Game no longer requests ads and does not start the Google Mobile Ads SDK in later sessions.

Google and its partners may process IP addresses and IP-derived approximate location, device and advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), app and ad interactions, impressions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals to serve, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Advertising data may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners. On iOS the Game also supports Apple's SKAdNetwork, which lets ad networks measure ad-driven installs without identifying you. We do not operate an independent database of players' advertising identifiers. Google and its partners handle data under their applicable policies: Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Before requesting any ad, the Game asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether a consent message applies in your region; this request sends technical information such as your IP address, device and app details to Google. Where applicable, UMP presents European consent notices and US state privacy choices. You may accept, refuse or manage available advertising choices in those notices as required by applicable law. Where UMP requires an in-app privacy-options entry point, it appears in the Game's Settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; contextual or other permitted ads may still appear. Advertising consent is separate from the optional gameplay-analytics and crash-report settings described below.

On iOS, before showing ads, the Game may display a Google consent message followed by Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. Only if you allow tracking may Google and its partners use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize and measure ads across apps. If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not available and ads may still be shown without it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. ATT permission and UMP advertising choices are separate controls.

3. In-app purchases

The Game may offer a one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently removes ads, including ads added in future versions. Apple processes iOS payments through StoreKit / Apple In-App Purchase; if offered on Android, Google processes payments through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment-card number. The Game checks verified transaction or entitlement status to grant and restore ad removal. Apple or Google may process purchases, transactions and fraud-prevention information under their respective policies. The Game also uses Apple's verified app-transaction information on the device to tell App Store and TestFlight installations apart; this does not send your purchase details to us.

4. Leaderboards and platform services

The iOS Game uses Apple Game Center for optional live leaderboards. When you are signed in to Game Center and a qualifying result is submitted, Apple may process your platform player identifier, display name, leaderboard identifier, result and related game context. Current leaderboard results include total damage for Score Attack or Weekly Challenge, or a Boss Challenge completion time; the leaderboard context records which Boss was played and the run's longest chain. Only results that pass the Game's on-device result check are submitted, and only for the Game Center account that was signed in when the run started. Public leaderboard entries, rankings and Game Center display names may be visible to other Game Center users under Apple's settings and visibility rules. At launch the Game may show Apple's Game Center sign-in; you can dismiss it and play without signing in. Viewing rankings downloads other players' Game Center display names and scores from Apple.

If Google Play Games Services leaderboards are enabled in a future Android release, Google may similarly process your Play Games identifier, display name, results and game context under its policies. The Game does not currently operate its own player-account service, chat service or leaderboard backend. Signing out of a platform account or uninstalling the Game does not by itself delete a result already held by that platform; manage platform data and deletion requests through Apple or Google as applicable.

5. Optional gameplay analytics — Firebase Analytics

Supported iOS distribution builds include Google Firebase Analytics for product improvement. Gameplay analytics is off by default and is controlled separately by the optional “Share gameplay analytics” switch in the Game's Settings. The Game's custom gameplay events are sent only while this option is enabled and the service is available. Firebase is started only in App Store and TestFlight builds whose installation Apple's StoreKit verifies. Development, simulator, test and other builds never start Firebase.

Events describe: tutorial start and completion; run start and end (game mode, difficulty, rules version, active play time, whether the run was completed or quit, total damage, longest chain, perfect counters, whether the Golden Core was earned, the outcome, the Boss kill time where applicable, and whether the result passed the Game's on-device check); the play-again action; interstitial ad lifecycle steps (offered, requested, loaded, shown, closed, failed or unavailable); views of the Remove Ads offer and purchase or restore outcomes; and taps on the share button. Each event also records whether the build came from the App Store or TestFlight. These events are for understanding engagement, difficulty balance, reliability and monetization performance. A share tap is not proof that sharing was completed. We do not intentionally attach your name, email, feedback text or a developer account ID to these events.

When enabled, Firebase may also process SDK-generated information such as an app-instance or installation identifier, device and operating-system details, app version, sessions, approximate geographic information inferred from network data, event timestamps, and events the Firebase SDK records automatically, such as first open, session start, engagement time, app updates and, where applicable, in-app purchase and ad-impression events. This information may be pseudonymous rather than completely anonymous. The iOS Firebase configuration disables IDFV collection, analytics-based ad-personalization signals and automatic screen reporting. The Game uses the Firebase Analytics library without advertising-identifier (IDFA) support, sets Google's advertising-storage, ad-user-data and ad-personalization consent signals to denied, and sets no user ID; we do not use Firebase Analytics for cross-app advertising tracking. You can turn off gameplay analytics in Settings. The Game disables further analytics collection and requests a reset of its analytics data on withdrawal; this cannot recall data already sent to Google.

6. Optional crash reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

Supported iOS distribution builds also include Google Firebase Crashlytics to help diagnose crashes. Its “Share crash reports” choice is off by default and independent of gameplay analytics and advertising consent. Automatic crash-report uploading is disabled in the Game. With your opt-in, previously unsent reports stored on the device may be sent on a subsequent launch; without opt-in, the Game requests deletion of unsent reports when it makes the upload decision. Switching the option off stops further authorized sending and requests deletion of unsent reports, but cannot retract reports already transmitted or queued for transmission. A change to “Share crash reports” takes effect from the Game's next launch.

Crash reports may contain stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device model and technical characteristics, timestamps, session information, Crashlytics/Firebase installation identifiers, whether the build came from the App Store or TestFlight and, if gameplay analytics is also on, recent analytics events recorded before the crash. Such data helps diagnose and prioritize faults. We do not intentionally add your name or the contents of optional Tally feedback to crash reports. For Firebase service practices, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

We use information to operate gameplay and local records, provide platform leaderboards and purchase entitlements, display and measure ads when enabled, diagnose problems and prevent abuse, analyze optional gameplay metrics, and respond to feedback or support requests. Depending on the features you use, relevant recipients include Apple (Game Center and StoreKit), Google (AdMob, UMP, Firebase and potentially Google Play services) and Tally (feedback forms). These providers may act as service providers or handle certain information under their own privacy policies.

Where required by applicable law, processing is based on performance of the service you request, your consent for optional analytics or crash-report sharing and for advertising purposes that require consent, legitimate interests such as security and responding to requests where legally permitted, or legal obligations. You may withdraw consent for consent-based processing without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

We do not sell personal information directly to data brokers. Advertising-related disclosures to Google and its partners may nevertheless qualify as a “sale,” “sharing” or use for targeted advertising under some US state privacy laws. Where those laws apply, use the relevant UMP privacy notice or in-app Privacy options to exercise available opt-out rights, or contact us. If no Privacy options entry is shown, no advertising choice applies to you, or you have removed ads. We may disclose information if lawfully required or necessary to protect the security of the Game and its users.

8. Retention and security

Local records generally remain until you remove app data or uninstall the Game, subject to device backups. Support emails and feedback are kept as long as reasonably necessary to respond, improve the Game or satisfy legal obligations; you may ask us to review or delete information we directly control. Data already provided to Apple or Google for purchases, rankings or advertising is subject to those providers' retention rules and your platform settings.

Firebase Analytics event data is kept for 2 months under our Google Analytics data-retention setting; Crashlytics crash reports are kept for about 90 days under Firebase's published practices. We delete feedback submissions and support emails when they are no longer needed to respond or improve the Game, and in any case within 24 months. Turning off either optional setting does not automatically erase all information previously received by a provider. Tally maintains its own technical records under its notice. We use standard security measures and encrypted connections where supported, but no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy choices and rights

You can change the optional gameplay-analytics and crash-report switches in the Game's Settings when those functions are available. Where required by UMP, Settings also provides “Privacy options” to review applicable advertising choices. iOS tracking permission is managed separately through the system Tracking settings. On Android, where supported, you can manage your advertising identifier and Google Play privacy preferences. You may also manage your Game Center or Google Play Games information and purchase history using the respective platform's tools.

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to processing of personal information, and to opt out of certain advertising uses. You may withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. For information controlled directly by the developer, email the contact below with enough details to locate the relevant record; do not send passwords or payment-card details. Because the Game has no player accounts and does not show your analytics identifier, we usually cannot identify your Firebase analytics or crash data from a request. Turning off “Share gameplay analytics” stops collection and resets the identifier on your device; Google deletes analytics data under the retention period described in Section 8. Some requests concerning platform accounts or advertising-network data may need to be made to Apple, Google or another responsible provider. We will respond to requests as applicable law requires.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country), and ads are not configured as child-directed. Additional protections or higher age thresholds may apply under local laws and the Game's store-audience settings. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us.
